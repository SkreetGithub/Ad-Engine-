# How to Make a Real Ad Post – Walkthrough

## 1. Paying for ads (Meta account / money)

- **You don’t “add money” to Meta like a prepaid balance.** You add a **payment method** (credit/debit card) to your **Meta ad account**. Meta then **charges that card** as your ads run (e.g. daily based on your daily budget).
- **Where to set it up:** [Meta Ads Manager](https://business.facebook.com/adsmanager) → **Billing** (or **Payment settings**). Add a payment method there. Same place you added it when you did “Payment method” in “Get started with Ads Manager.”
- **How it works:** When a campaign is **Active**, Meta spends up to your **daily budget** and bills your card. No need to pre-load funds; they charge based on actual spend.

### Never go over your total budget (priority)
- Go to **Settings** → **Budget Guardrails** and set **Max total budget ($)**. Save it.
- When you use **Quick Launch** or **New Campaign**, set **Max total budget** (or leave it to use the value from Settings). Facebook will **stop the campaign** when that amount is spent, so you never exceed your budget, get unexpected charges, or risk card declines or account issues.

---

## 1b. Max total budget (so you never go over)

- In **Settings** → **Budget Guardrails**, set **Max total budget ($)** and click Save. This value is used when you launch campaigns so Facebook stops spending when that amount is reached — no surprise charges or card declines.
- In **Campaigns** → **Quick Launch**, the **Max total budget ($)** field is at the top; when set, the campaign uses this cap instead of daily budget. Same for **New Campaign** (optional Max total budget field).

## 2. Step-by-step: create a real ad from the Campaign Manager

### Step 1: Open the form
- Go to **Campaigns** in the sidebar.
- Click the green **New Campaign** button so the “Launch New Campaign” form is visible.

### Step 2: Campaign name
- In **Campaign Name**, enter a short name (e.g. `Unique Pickups – Feb Leads`). This is for your reference in Ads Manager.

### Step 3: Destination URL
- **Destination URL** is where people go when they click the ad (e.g. your signup or booking page). It’s already set to `https://www.uniquepickups.com`; change it if you want a specific page (e.g. a contact or “Get started” page).

### Step 4: Caption / copy (the ad text)
- **Option A – Write it yourself:** Type your ad message in **Caption / Copy** (the big text box). Keep it short and clear (e.g. 1–3 sentences).
- **Option B – Use AI:** Click the green **AI Generate Copy** button. The app uses your **Campaign name** and **Strategy** (if you picked one) to generate suggested ad copy and puts it in **Caption / Copy**. You can edit the result before launching.

### Step 5: Strategy (optional but helpful for AI)
- In **Strategy Theater**, choose a strategy (e.g. “Flash Sale Urgency,” “Social Proof / Testimonial”). This helps the **AI Generate Copy** button match the style of copy to your goal.

### Step 6: Daily budget
- Set **Daily Budget** (e.g. $5–10 to start). This is the **max** Meta will spend per day on this campaign when it’s active. The “Projected ROI” box updates as you change the slider.

### Step 7: Launch the campaign
- Click the green **Launch Campaign** button. The app creates a **real** campaign in your Meta ad account in **PAUSED** state (so it doesn’t spend until you turn it on).

### Step 8: Turn the ad on in Meta (so it actually runs and spends)
- Open [Meta Ads Manager](https://business.facebook.com/adsmanager).
- Find your new campaign (same name you entered).
- Switch its status from **Paused** to **Active**.  
- Once it’s **Active**, Meta will start delivering the ad and will charge your **payment method on file** (see section 1) for the spend, up to your daily budget.

---

## 3. What “AI Generate Copy” does

- **AI Generate Copy** creates the **text** for your ad (the caption/copy). It uses your **Campaign name** and **Strategy** (if set) to suggest short, punchy ad copy.
- After you click it, the generated text appears in **Caption / Copy**. You can edit it before clicking **Launch Campaign**. The **information** (message) people see on the ad is this caption plus the destination link; the campaign name is only for your organization in Ads Manager.

## Quick checklist

| Step | What to do |
|------|------------|
| Payment | Add a card in Ads Manager → Billing so Meta can charge for spend. |
| Name | Fill **Campaign Name**. |
| URL | Set **Destination URL** (default: uniquepickups.com). |
| Copy | Write in **Caption / Copy** or use **AI Generate Copy**, then edit if needed. |
| Budget | Set **Daily Budget**. For a hard cap so you never exceed a total, set **Max total budget** in Settings and in Quick Launch or New Campaign. |
| Launch | Click **Launch Campaign** (creates campaign in **Paused** state). |
| Go live | In Ads Manager, set the campaign to **Active** so it runs and pays from your card. |
